# Tick List

*Lists you tick off: groceries, chores, work. Flag what matters, see what you added today, and share a list with the people who shop from it.*

- **Open it:** [iOS](charmiq://./iOS.md). Home-screen setup: [Mobile](charmiq://../README.md).
- **Your lists** (`lists.json`, the Application's app-content): every list and its items. Share the document as an Editor and everyone on it sees each tick as it happens; two people changing the list at once both keep their change.
- **Where you left off** (the Application's app-state): the list or view you had open.
