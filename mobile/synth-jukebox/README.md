# Synth Jukebox

*A music library whose every album is composed and played live on your phone, from its genre and a seed: no audio files, no streaming. Compose a new album whenever you like.*

- **Open it:** [iOS](charmiq://./iOS.md). Home-screen setup: [Mobile](charmiq://../README.md).
- **Your library** (`library.json`, the Application's app-content): the albums and your Loved songs. Everyone you share the document with sees the same library.
- **Where you left off** (the Application's app-state): what is playing, the queue, position, volume and the tab you were on. Launch it again and it picks up there.
- **Sound:** tap play to start; iOS starts audio only from a tap. It plays even with the phone in Silent mode.
